The University of St. Thomas in St. Paul, Minnesota, and is a private Catholic univesrity that was founded in 1885. The university is known for its strong sense of community and its commitment to social justice. They wanted to bring together their newsroom and magazine publications to create a place where students, faculty, and staff can read all their stories and experiences in a single location.

Some of the main goals for this project were to create a site to bring these two souces together under one roof. The site needed to be easy to navigate and accessible to all users, including those with disabilities. The design needed to be modern and clean, with a focus on typography and imagery. Most importantly, the site needed to be easy for the editorial team to create and manage content that matched their vision.

Built using WordPress and the Gutenberg block editor, the design included custom blocks including: featured stories, layouts, gallery, and more. All of these are editable in the block editor and is 1:1 with the front end, making it easy for the editorial team to see what the final product will look like before publishing.
